# Changes

## 0.2.0
- 23 effects in five groups (color, motion, nature, typing, music), each with its own settings.
- Heat map and trail also as layers on top of any effect, optionally over highlighted keys.
- Effects with several colors get a brightness per color; still effects at speed 0.
- A color of 1 or more never dims to fully off.
- Sound helper records left and right for the stereo effect.
- Profiles from 0.1 move over by themselves: the base color becomes the effect "Color / gradient". Going back to 0.1 afterwards
  moves the settings file aside, so keep a copy if you might.

## 0.1.0
First version: waves, pressed keys, base color, highlights, gradients, PC stats, sound and microphone display, other lights,
profiles, macros, settings page, installers for Linux, macOS and Windows.
